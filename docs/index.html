Privacy Policy, Loop Me

Last updated: 27 October 2026 Applies to: Loop Me for iPhone (com.loopmescamsafety.app), version 1.0.1 and later

The short version

Loop Me collects nothing, and has no server.

Every message you check is read and answered on your own iPhone. The only thing that ever leaves your phone is what you choose to send to one family member, and it travels through your own iCloud account, not through us. We cannot see it, because there is nowhere for it to go.

If that is all you wanted to know, you can stop reading here.

What we collect

Nothing. Not your name, not your phone number, not your contacts, not the messages you check, not how often you use the app, not your location, not an advertising identifier, not a crash report, not an analytics event.

We do not use any third-party analytics, advertising, attribution, or crash reporting service. We have no server, so there is nothing for anything to be sent to.

What stays on your phone

Loop Me stores a few things locally, in your iPhone's own app storage, so it can work between launches:

WhatWhy
Your first nameSo notes to family say who they are from
Trusted contacts you addSo you can reach them in one tap
Your check historySo you can look back at what you have checked
Your family code wordSo the app can remind you to ask for it
Numbers you blocked, senders you trustSo the text filter and call directory know them
Your settingsTo remember your preferences

All of this lives only on your device. It is included in your encrypted iPhone backup if you use one, and it is deleted permanently when you delete the app. We never receive any of it.

Your family code word is never transmitted, never included in a shared check, and never leaves your phone.

Phone numbers, email addresses and long account-style numbers inside a message are blanked out before the message is stored in history or shown to family.

The family link

If you choose to link one family member, Loop Me uses Apple's iCloud (CloudKit) to carry two things between your two phones:

  • a check that was flagged on your phone: a shortened, blanked copy of the message, the reason it was flagged, when, and who it was from;
  • notes the family member writes back.

These records are stored in your own iCloud account, in a private area that only the one person you invited can see. They are not stored on any server of ours, we have no account that can read them, and Apple's iCloud terms cover them. Either of you can end the link from inside the app, which removes the other phone's access at once.

The family member receives the invitation as an ordinary text message that you send yourself from the Messages app, exactly as if you had typed it.

When the family link is not set up, "Loop in" writes a plain text message and hands it to Messages for you to send. Nothing else is sent anywhere.

The text filter, the call directory and the share sheet

Loop Me includes three small iPhone extensions. You switch each one on yourself in the iPhone Settings app, and each one runs entirely on your phone:

  • Text filter. When you turn it on under Messages, iOS shows Loop Me each text from a number that is not in your contacts, before it reaches you. Loop Me reads it on the phone and tells iOS whether it is junk. The text is not sent anywhere, and texts from people in your contacts are never shown to Loop Me at all.
  • Call directory. When you turn it on under Phone, iOS reads a list of numbers you blocked, and numbers that sent a flagged text, so those calls are blocked or labeled. The list is built from your own history and never downloaded.
  • Share sheet. When you share a message, link or screenshot to Loop Me from another app, it is checked on the phone and the answer is shown right there.

What these extensions see is kept in a storage area shared only between them and the app, on your phone.

Permissions Loop Me asks for

Loop Me asks only when you tap something that needs it, and each one is used for that purpose alone:

  • Camera. Only when you choose to photograph a message. The photo is read on your phone and is not uploaded or stored by us.
  • Photos. Only when you pick a screenshot to check. Read on your phone, not uploaded.
  • Contacts. Only when you choose to add a trusted family member or pick who to invite. Your contacts are not read otherwise, not copied, and not uploaded.
  • Microphone and Speech Recognition. Only when you tap "Speak it". Your speech is turned into text using Apple's on-device recognition. Nothing is uploaded.
  • Notifications. To show a flagged check or a family note on the other phone, and to remind you that a check is still open. The family notifications are delivered through Apple's push service as part of iCloud; their content is written by your own phone.

You can withdraw any of these at any time in Settings, Loop Me on your iPhone. The app keeps working without them; the features that need them simply ask again.

Artificial intelligence

Loop Me's scam detection runs entirely on your iPhone. Where your device supports it, Loop Me uses Apple's on-device intelligence and a small model bundled inside the app. No message text is ever sent to us, to Apple, to OpenAI, or to any other company for analysis.

Reporting a scam

If you choose to report a message, Loop Me either hands a text to the Messages app addressed to your carrier's spam number (7726), or copies a short write-up to your clipboard and opens the FTC's reporting page in your browser. You decide whether to send either one. Loop Me does not report anything on its own.

Children

Loop Me is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect anything from anyone, of any age.

Your rights

Laws such as the GDPR and the CCPA give you rights to access, correct, export, or delete the personal data a company holds about you.

We hold no data about you, so there is nothing for us to access, export, or delete. To erase everything Loop Me has stored on your own device, delete the app; iOS removes its storage completely. Records in your iCloud account are yours, and ending the family link inside the app removes the shared area.

We do not sell or share personal information, because we do not have any.
