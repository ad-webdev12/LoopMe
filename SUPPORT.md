# Loop Me — Help

**Loop Me for iPhone · version 1.0.0**

Loop Me checks a suspicious text or call for you, tells you plainly whether it
looks like a scam, and helps you ask someone you trust. It works offline, has no
account, and keeps nothing on any server.

## Getting help

**[REPLACE WITH YOUR SUPPORT EMAIL]**

We aim to reply within two working days. It helps if you tell us which iPhone
you have and what version of iOS it is running (**Settings → General → About**).

Please do **not** email us the scam message itself if it contains your bank
details, a code, or anything personal. Describing what happened is enough.

## Common questions

### How do I check a message?

Open Loop Me and paste the message in, or use the **Share** button in Messages
and choose Loop Me. Tap **Check**. The answer comes back in under a second.

### Why can't Loop Me read my texts automatically?

iPhone does not allow any app to read your messages. Apple only permits
filtering of texts from numbers that are not in your contacts, which you switch
on yourself in **Settings → Apps → Messages → Unknown & Junk**. iMessage and
WhatsApp are sealed off from every app, so for those, use **Share**.

This is an Apple rule, not a limitation we chose, and it applies to every app of
this kind.

### Does Loop Me listen to my phone calls?

No. iPhone gives no app access to the audio of a live call, so no app can do
this — including ours. The **practice call** in Loop Me is a rehearsal: a
scripted run-through of the commonest scam call, so the pattern is familiar
before a real one arrives.

### What is the family code word?

A single word only your family knows. If someone calls sounding like a relative
and asks for money or urgency, ask them for the word. Someone using a recording
or an imitation of a familiar voice will not have it.

Set it in **Settings → Your family code word**. It is stored only on your phone
and is never sent anywhere — not even in a check you share with family.

### How does looping in a family member work?

Loop Me writes a text message for you and opens Messages with it ready to send.
You send it yourself, like any other text. Your family member taps the link and
their copy of Loop Me opens to the right screen so they can reply.

Nothing passes through us. There is no server.

### Do I need an account or the internet?

Neither. Loop Me works with the phone in aeroplane mode. There is nothing to
sign up for.

### I think I have already been caught by a scam. What do I do?

Open Loop Me and tap **I think I've been scammed**. It walks you through the
five things to do, in order — starting with the reminder that this happens to
millions of careful people every year, and that it is not your fault.

You can also report it at [reportfraud.ftc.gov](https://reportfraud.ftc.gov).

### Why did Loop Me say a real message was suspicious?

No checker is perfect. If a sender you trust is being flagged, add them under
**Settings → Senders you trust** and Loop Me will stop warning about them.

If you think we got something badly wrong, please tell us — describe the kind of
message, not its contents, and we will look at it.

### How do I delete everything?

Delete the app. iOS removes everything Loop Me stored, permanently. There is
nothing held anywhere else.

## Privacy

Loop Me collects nothing at all. The full policy is in
[PRIVACY.md](PRIVACY.md).

## Accessibility

Loop Me follows your iPhone's text size, including the largest accessibility
sizes, and every screen works with VoiceOver. If something is hard to read or
hard to reach, please tell us — that is a bug worth fixing.
